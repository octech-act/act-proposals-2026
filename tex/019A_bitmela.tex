{\bfseries Subject: Proposal for reimbursement of travel expenses for Bitmela Launchpad 2026 finalists}

Respected Ma'am,

\textbf{DevlUp Labs, IIT Jodhpur} proposes the reimbursement of travel expenses for a team of \textbf{five students of IIT Jodhpur} who have been selected as finalists in the \textbf{Bitmela Launchpad 2026}.

\textbf{Bitmela} is a festival focused on the intersection of \textbf{money, technology, Bitcoin and AI}, being held in Jaipur on \textbf{30th--31st October 2026}. The Bitmela Launchpad is an ideathon in which selected teams receive mentorship and develop their ideas into working prototypes before presenting them on the main stage in Jaipur. The 2026 Launchpad has five finalist teams and provides four weeks of mentorship leading up to the final pitch.

\subhead{Participation in Bitmela Launchpad}
The IIT Jodhpur team has been selected in the \textbf{``Agentic Payments''} track of the Launchpad. The track focuses on building \textbf{AI agents capable of conducting transactions using Bitcoin, UPI, or both}, with applications including autonomous commerce, agents paying for their own compute, and machine-to-machine payments.

The finalist teams present their projects on the \textbf{Bitmela main stage in Jaipur on 30th October 2026}, and the winners are announced on 31st October 2026. Participation will give the students an opportunity to \textbf{develop their project under mentorship, interact with builders, investors and industry professionals, and showcase their work on a national platform} focused on Bitcoin, AI and emerging payment technologies.
