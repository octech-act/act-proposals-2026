\subhead{Justification for the proposed route}
\begin{itemize}
  \item There is only one direct train from Jodhpur in the travel window, the 15623 Bhagat Ki Kothi--Kamakhya Express, and it reaches Kamakhya at 22:00 on 10th December, after the Opening Ceremony, with only 60 3A berths available. The other direct train, the 15631 Barmer--Guwahati Express, reaches 1.5 days before the accommodation begins.
  \item Of the trains from Delhi, the North East Express has no confirmed berths (3A on RAC), and the 12424 Rajdhani Express is already waitlisted.
  \item The Brahmaputra Mail has only 29 3A berths, far short of the 91 needed, and the only Jodhpur train that reaches Delhi before it leaves arrives at a different station 75 minutes before departure, which is not a safe connection for 91 members.
  \item The 20504 Rajdhani Express is therefore the only option with confirmed berths for the full contingent. It reaches Guwahati on 10th December, within the travel window and without additional accommodation charges, and its fare includes meals for the 31-hour journey.
  \item For the Jodhpur -- Delhi leg, the 22481 Express has the most available berths (199) and arrives at 05:20, leaving a buffer of six hours before the Rajdhani departs. The 22491 Mandore Express (124 berths, \rs~1,020, arriving 06:45 at Delhi Jn.) and the 15623 Express (130 berths, \rs~985, arriving 04:40 at Delhi Jn.) are alternatives.
\end{itemize}

The students will arrange their return journey after the event.
