{\centering{\Large\bfseries Selection Process, Inter IIT Tech Meet 15.0}\par}

This document describes how the IIT Jodhpur contingent for the \textbf{Inter-IIT Tech Meet 15.0} is being selected. Selection is carried out top-down: the administration and leadership of the contingent are put in place first, and is then responsible for building the talent pool and selecting the teams for each problem statement.

\society{1. Contingent Administration}
As per the Inter-IIT Tech Meet constitution, the administration of each contingent consists of the \textbf{Contingent Leader (CL)}, the \textbf{Deputy Contingent Leaders (DCLs)} and the \textbf{Technical Secretary}. At IIT Jodhpur, the General Secretary, ACT Council (\GSname) is part of the administration as the Technical Secretary. The CL and DCLs are selected as described below.

\society{2. Contingent Leader (CL)}
Interviews for the post of Contingent Leader were conducted on \textbf{23rd September, 2026}. Based on interview performance and overall suitability for the role, \textbf{Krish Teckchandani (B23CS1092)} was selected as the CL. The selection considered the candidate's vision, leadership capabilities, communication skills, and ability to contribute to the planning, coordination and overall functioning of the contingent.

\society{3. Deputy Contingent Leaders (DCLs)}
As per precedence, the posts of DCL are filled by the technical office bearers of the ACT Council. This year, there are two Technical Overall Coordinators (OCs) for the two posts of DCL, and hence no separate interviews were required. The following were proposed as DCLs:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Akhil Dhyani (B24CS1005)
  \item Mayank Bansal (B24CH1022)
\end{itemize}

\society{5. Talent Pool}
Once the leadership was in place, a form was floated to the student body for students interested in being part of the contingent, with each student indicating their preferred domain.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Test (7th October, 2026):} A test was conducted for the various domains in two slots of one hour each.
  \item \textbf{Interviews (8th October, 2026):} Candidates were interviewed based on their test performance and past experience. The final Talent Pool list was released after the interviews.
  \item \textbf{Final call (14th October, 2026):} A final call for the Talent Pool was released for students who missed the first round.
\end{itemize}

\society{6. Final Team Selection}
Problem statements for the Inter-IIT Tech Meet are released over time. As and when each problem statement is released, the team for it is selected from the Talent Pool by a panel of the Domain Leaders, Deputy Contingent Leaders and the Contingent Leader. Selection for each problem statement is based on the candidates' understanding of that problem statement.
